A Story

In a path in the woods, there was an adventurer.

She had traveled from far lands for the last 2 years, embarking on a quest.

These woods would be the last of her destination, for somewhere in these woods was a treasure.

Now this treasure was only told in books and fairy tales, and few actually traveled in search of this treasure.

No one knew what exactly the treasure was or what it did, but almost every story about it describes it in the same way.

An artifact that had the power to manifest the bearer’s any desire, and any wish that person may have, will come true.

As she continued down the path, a glimmer came into view, just beyond the foliage.

She walked further down to see a lamppost, or at least what was left of it.

The metal had been corroded to the point where its base just barely blended in with the dirt, and whatever material the lamp was made of was decayed beyond recognition.

It was a miracle the lamp was still lit at all, and by whom lit this disheveled lamp in the first place? It didn’t matter, such small intricacies were not worth much thought, there was a job to do.
